# VPN & Network Connectivity

## Category
network

## Quick Answer
For VPN issues, confirm your internet connection works, restart the VPN client, and re-enter your employee ID and network password. For office WiFi issues, forget and reconnect to the network. To reset your VPN password, visit vpn.company.com/reset.

## Common Problems & Solutions

### Cannot Connect to Office WiFi
**Cause:** Outdated network driver, incorrect password, or a temporary WiFi network issue.
**Solution:**
1. Forget the WiFi network on your device and reconnect using SSID "CompanyNet-5G".
2. Check that your network adapter driver is up to date.
3. Restart your laptop.
4. If the issue persists, raise a ticket under category "Network Issues".

### Cannot Connect Remotely / VPN Not Working
**Cause:** Employee is working from home or a remote location and cannot access internal systems.
**Solution:**
1. Confirm you are connected to the internet (try loading any website).
2. Download the VPN client from vpn.company.com/download if not already installed.
3. Log in using your employee ID and network password.
4. Ensure the VPN is active before accessing internal tools, shared drives, or internal websites.

### Resetting VPN Password
**Cause:** Forgotten VPN password or expired credentials.
**Solution:**
1. Visit vpn.company.com/reset.
2. Enter your employee ID.
3. An OTP will be sent to your registered company email.
4. Set a new password at least 10 characters long with one special character.
5. If the OTP does not arrive within 5 minutes, contact IT helpdesk at extension 4422.

### VPN Client Disconnects Frequently
**Cause:** Outdated VPN client, unstable network, or VPN server load during peak hours.
**Solution:**
1. Confirm the VPN client is up to date.
2. Restart the VPN client and reconnect.
3. Check VPN server status on the admin dashboard.
4. If on public or restricted WiFi, try switching networks or using a mobile hotspot.
5. If the issue affects multiple users, escalate to the network team — the VPN gateway may be down.

### Internet Proxy / Blocked Websites
**Cause:** All internet traffic routes through the company proxy, which may block certain sites by default.
**Solution:**
1. Proxy settings are auto-configured via WPAD and should not normally need manual changes.
2. If a website needed for work is blocked, submit an access request at tools.company.com under category "Proxy Whitelist Request" with a business justification.

## Escalation
Contact IT Service Desk if:
- VPN fails across multiple networks or devices.
- Multiple employees report the same connectivity issue at the same time.
- The account is repeatedly locked while attempting to connect.
