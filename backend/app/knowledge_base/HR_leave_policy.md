HR Leave Policy Guide
Version 1.0 | HR Department | 2025

1. Annual Leave Policy
Employees must apply for annual leave through the Employee Portal under Leave and Attendance. Select Apply Leave, choose the leave type and dates, attach supporting documents when required, and submit the request. The request is sent to the employee's reporting manager for approval. Track the request under My Requests.

2. Leave Request Not Submitting
Issue: Employee is unable to submit a leave request through the Employee Portal.
Possible Causes:
- Insufficient leave balance for the requested dates
- Dates overlap with another existing request
- Required information or supporting documents are missing
- The portal is experiencing a temporary outage
Solution / Steps to Resolve:
1 Check your current leave balance in the portal before applying
2 Confirm the dates do not overlap with an existing request
3 Ensure all required fields and supporting documents are attached
4 If the portal itself is unavailable, wait and retry, or report a possible outage to IT

3. Leave Request Stuck in Pending Status
Issue: Submitted leave request remains in pending status for an extended period.
Possible Causes:
- Awaiting manager approval
- Awaiting HR approval after manager sign-off
- Approver is on leave or unavailable
Solution / Steps to Resolve:
1 Check the current status under My Requests in the Employee Portal
2 If pending beyond 2-3 business days, follow up directly with your reporting manager
3 If still unresolved, escalate to HR Operations

4. Leave Balance Appears Incorrect
Issue: The leave balance shown in the portal does not match the employee's expectation.
Possible Causes:
- Recently taken leave not yet reflected in the system
- System sync delay between attendance and leave modules
- Carry-forward calculation from the previous cycle not applied correctly
Solution / Steps to Resolve:
1 Cross-check recent leave requests and their approval status
2 Allow 24-48 hours for the system to sync after a recent approval
3 If the discrepancy remains, report it to HR Operations with the specific dates in question

5. Cancelling a Leave Request
Issue: Employee needs to cancel a leave request that was already submitted or approved.
Possible Causes:
- Change in personal plans after submission
- Leave dates need to be rescheduled
Solution / Steps to Resolve:
1 Open My Requests in the Employee Portal
2 Select the leave request and choose Cancel
3 If the leave was already approved and the date has passed, contact HR Operations directly for manual correction

6. Sick Leave Policy
Employees are entitled to sick leave as per company policy. For sick leave exceeding 2 consecutive days, a medical certificate may be required to be uploaded in the portal. Sick leave should be reported as early as possible, ideally before the start of the working day.

7. Leave Eligibility
Annual leave and sick leave eligibility applies to full-time employees. Contractors and interns should refer to their specific employment agreement for applicable leave terms, as this policy may not apply to their category.

8. Leave Approval Hierarchy
All leave requests are routed to the employee's reporting manager first. For leave exceeding 5 consecutive days, additional approval from HR Operations is required after manager approval.

9. Escalation
Contact HR Operations if:
- Leave balance appears incorrect after verification
- A request remains pending beyond the standard processing time
- The wrong leave type was applied to a request
- A policy exception is required
