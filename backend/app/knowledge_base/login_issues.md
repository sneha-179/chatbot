# Login & Account Access

## Category
login

## Quick Answer
If you cannot log in, first try the Forgot Password link on the login page. If your account is locked, wait 15 minutes or contact IT. If you are not receiving an OTP, check your registered email and spam folder before contacting the helpdesk.

## Common Problems & Solutions

### Forgotten Password
**Cause:** Employee does not remember their current password, or confused personal and work credentials.
**Solution:**
1. Click the "Forgot Password" link on the login page.
2. Verify your identity using your registered email or employee ID.
3. A time-limited password reset link will be sent to your registered company email.
4. Set a new password following the company policy: minimum 8 characters, 1 uppercase letter, 1 number, and 1 special character.

### Account Locked After Multiple Failed Attempts
**Cause:** The system locks an account after 3-5 incorrect login attempts, either from a genuine mistake or a possible unauthorized access attempt.
**Solution:**
1. Wait 15 minutes for the automatic lockout to clear, or
2. Contact IT to confirm the lockout in the admin panel and manually unlock the account.
3. Verify your identity before the lockout is cleared.
4. If unauthorized access is suspected, change your password immediately after regaining access.

### OTP Not Received During Login
**Cause:** Email delivery delay, incorrect registered email, or spam filtering.
**Solution:**
1. Wait up to 5 minutes for the OTP to arrive.
2. Check your spam or junk folder.
3. Confirm your registered email address is correct in your profile.
4. If the OTP still does not arrive, contact IT helpdesk at extension 4422.

### Password Expired
**Cause:** Company policy requires password rotation every 60-90 days.
**Solution:**
1. You will be prompted to reset your password automatically on login.
2. Follow the "reset expired password" flow instead of the standard reset.
3. Set a new password meeting the complexity requirements.
4. Confirm the new expiry date is reflected correctly after reset.

### Incorrect Username or Employee ID
**Cause:** Using a personal email instead of the official company ID, or a typo in the employee ID.
**Solution:**
1. Confirm you are using your official company employee ID, not a personal email.
2. Double-check for typos.
3. If the account was recently created, allow up to 24 hours for provisioning to complete.

### Browser Cache or Cookie Issues
**Cause:** Corrupted cached login session, browser extensions interfering, or cookies disabled.
**Solution:**
1. Clear browser cache and cookies for the login site.
2. Try logging in via an incognito or private browsing window.
3. Ensure cookies are enabled for the company domain in browser privacy settings.
4. If the issue persists, try an alternate supported browser.

### Frequent Session Timeout or Repeated Logout
**Cause:** Aggressive session timeout policy, multiple simultaneous logins from different devices, or system clock out of sync.
**Solution:**
1. Check whether you are logged in from multiple devices simultaneously and log out of unused sessions.
2. Confirm your device's date and time are set correctly.
3. If timeouts continue happening within a normal session, contact IT.

## Escalation
Contact IT Helpdesk (extension 4422) if:
- The account remains locked after 15 minutes.
- OTP is not received after checking spam and confirming the registered email.
- The issue affects multiple employees at once (possible system-wide issue).
