# Payslip Download & Payroll Issues

## Category
payroll

## Quick Answer
To download your payslip, sign in to the Employee Portal, open Payroll, select My Payslips, choose the required month, and select Download PDF. If the payslip is missing or the PDF will not open, see the specific problem below.

## Common Problems & Solutions

### Payslip Not Available / Missing
**Cause:** Payroll processing for the selected month is not yet complete.
**Solution:**
1. Check the published payroll calendar for the expected availability date.
2. If the date has passed and the payslip is still missing, report the specific month to Payroll.
3. New joiners may see their first payslip only after the first full payroll cycle completes.

### Payslip PDF Not Opening / Corrupted
**Cause:** Browser issue, corrupted download, or a rendering error in the payslip generation service.
**Solution:**
1. Try downloading the file again, ideally on a different browser.
2. Try accessing from a different device.
3. Disable any browser extension that blocks downloads temporarily.
4. If the file is still garbled or in the wrong format (raw HTML or .txt instead of PDF), report the specific record and month to the Payroll or backend team so it can be regenerated.

### Bank Account Details Mismatch
**Cause:** Salary credited to an old or incorrect bank account, or the slip shows outdated account details.
**Solution:**
1. Do not ignore this — report it to Payroll immediately with your correct account details.
2. Payroll will verify and correct the account on file.
3. Confirm the updated details reflect correctly from the next payroll cycle.
4. If a payment was already sent to the wrong account, Payroll and Finance will coordinate to reverse and reprocess it.

### Previous Month's Payslip Not Available
**Cause:** Archival policy limits how far back payslips are visible on the self-service portal, or records were migrated during a system change.
**Solution:**
1. Confirm the organization's payslip retention and visibility window with Payroll.
2. If the record is in cold storage or a separate archive, Payroll can retrieve it.
3. If it cannot be restored to the portal, request it be shared securely via email.

### Payslip Email Notification Not Received
**Cause:** Email integration failure for that notification batch, or an incorrect/outdated registered email.
**Solution:**
1. Check that your registered email in the Employee Portal is correct and current.
2. Check spam or junk folders.
3. The payslip is still available for manual download from the portal even if the notification email did not arrive.

### Salary Amount Looks Incorrect
**Cause:** Changes to attendance, deductions, tax, benefits, overtime, or reimbursements can change the net amount month to month.
**Solution:**
1. Review the breakdown shown on the payslip for the specific change.
2. If the amount still appears incorrect after review, submit a Payroll Correction request through the Employee Portal with the affected pay period and a description of the issue.

## Escalation
Contact Payroll if:
- A payslip is missing after the published availability date.
- Salary or deductions appear incorrect.
- Bank account or personal details on the payslip are wrong.

Contact IT if:
- The portal does not load at all.
- The download fails for all documents, not just payslips.
- You receive a server or authentication error while trying to access Payroll.
